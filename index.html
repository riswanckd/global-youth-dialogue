About The Movement

About the Project

From a temporary debate tournament in Doha to a lasting global youth intellectual network.

Qatar ISDC7 Legacy

Our Origin Story

Young debaters from different countries met through the Qatar International Schools Debate Championship (ISDC7) in Doha.

Although the tournament concluded, delegates realized the urgent value of continuing to debate together, critically examine global affairs, learn from divergent cultural perspectives, and create platforms for other young thinkers.

Global Youth Dialogue was founded to turn that temporary international spark into a continuing, youth-led academic network.

Our Mission

To create an international space where young people can discuss global issues, challenge ideas, develop research and communication skills, and learn from peers across borders.

Our Vision

A connected generation of young people capable of engaging thoughtfully with global challenges and participating in meaningful international dialogue.

The Dialogue Cycle

How the Programme Works

A visual 5-step cycle converting spoken parliamentary debate into rigorous academic documentation.

01

01 — SELECT

A topic is selected and approved from community proposals across eight global categories.

02

02 — PREPARE

Speakers research the issue, assemble empirical dossiers, and prepare presentations.

03

03 — DISCUSS

The community meets online for a structured presentation, parliamentary debate, or dialogue.

04

04 — REFLECT

Members submit qualitative feedback scrutinizing arguments, evidence gaps, and overlooked angles.

05

05 — DOCUMENT

The session is recorded and converted into a written academic-style summary in our archive.
